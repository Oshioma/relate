## What each photo actually shows

- **hero.webp**: An older bearded man in a flat cap and plaid shirt waters potted plants (including a chili-pepper plant) with a metal watering can while a young girl watches, in a leafy garden on an overcast day. It differs from the brief in a few ways: the younger person is a small child, not a young adult; the setting is a leafy garden with potted plants, not rows of vegetables; and the light is soft and overcast, not sunny. The image is cropped from the top, so the lower part of the watering can is cut off.
- **stage-1-profile.webp**: A young woman with long dark hair, wearing a denim jumpsuit, works with seedlings in plug trays inside a polytunnel greenhouse (Pasture Song Farm, Pottstown, PA). It differs from the brief: the setting is a farm greenhouse, not a sunny balcony; the seedlings are in trays, not small pots; and her face is mostly hidden by her hair.
- **stage-2-mentor.webp**: A middle-aged woman in a plaid shirt and blue gloves crouches over a dense raised bed of beet greens, with onions or alliums behind. It differs from the brief: she is kneeling and working rather than standing, and nothing in the photo shows she is "experienced" or "organic".
- **stage-3-mission.webp**: A close-up of soil-covered hands working dark garden soil in a wooden raised bed dotted with tiny seedlings. It is a close match, although the hands are pressing the soil next to the seedlings rather than clearly setting one seedling into the ground.
- **stage-4-progress.webp**: A woman leans in to photograph a row of small potted plants (pink-leaved polka-dot plants and begonia-type plants in ceramic pots) with a smartphone, against a weathered wooden wall. It differs from the brief: the plants are ornamentals, not tomatoes, and the setting is a pavement or porch, not a garden.
- **stage-5-harvest.webp**: A smiling woman in a straw hat, grey shirt and green apron holds a white bowl of fresh produce (tomatoes, small peppers, red potatoes, beet greens and a marigold) on a porch surrounded by ferns. It differs from the brief: the produce is in a bowl, not a basket; there is no lettuce, and the round red vegetables look like red potatoes rather than radishes; and the light is soft shade, not direct sun.
