# True Calendar — Research Batch 06

**76 curated records:** 30 Māori named lunar nights (Ngāti Kahungunu/Te Ara), 30 Hawaiian named lunar nights (Kapiʻolani Community College), 4 Igbo market days (2022 academic paper), and 12 Māori seasonal months (Ngāi Tūhoe historical account).

## Important
- These are **source-linked research drafts**, not verified 2026 date matches. All date_status values are `undated_recurring_cycle`.
- Do not present any record as today’s exact traditional night without a community-specific anchor and date-conversion review.
- Māori lunar night ordering is a particular published variant; other iwi differ.
- Hawaiian day names repeat (e.g. ʻOlekūkahi); use ordinal cycle_position, not name, as the key.
- Hawaiian historical sources sometimes disagree on planting recommendations; keep source distinctions visible.
- Igbo market days vary in local anchor and activity; never infer a universal Eke/Orie/Afọ/Nkwọ date.
- Māori seasonal descriptions are New Zealand-specific and must not be translated directly to Zanzibar climate.
- Source texts are **paraphrased**, not reproduced. These records are additive to batches 01–05; deduplicate by underlying claim and source before reporting totals.
