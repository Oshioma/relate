# Research batch 10: the astrology traditions behind Personal Moon

Oshi asked (6 November 2026) for a Personal Moon layer: the Moon's passage
through the zodiac read against each member's Sun sign, with the historical
traditions kept apart. This batch holds what the traditions themselves say.
Personal Moon reads it; the history feed never shows it as day records
(`historyBundle` in research/daily.ts filters every `astrology.` key out).

## Four traditions, four different frames
- **Hellenistic** (Firmicus Maternus, *Mathesis* II.19, Latin ed. Kroll and
  Skutsch 1897, our English): the twelve places counted from the rising sign
  at birth. Place 12 is the Bad Daemon (enemies, illness), place 2 the "gate
  of the underworld" (growth of hope and possessions), place 1 life and spirit.
- **Medieval Arabic** (al-Bīrūnī, *Book of Instruction* §461, 1029, tr.
  Wright 1934): the twelve houses in nativities, counted from the ascendant.
- **Jyotiṣa** (Varāhamihira, *Bṛhat Saṃhitā*, transit chapter vv. 8–10, tr.
  Iyer 1884): the Moon's passage through each sign counted from the birth
  Moon sign, in the sidereal zodiac.
- **Modern Western** (Wikipedia, House (astrology)): houses counted from the
  Sun sign ("solar houses"), with modern astrologers' meanings.

Every record says which frame it uses. Only the modern one counts from the
Sun sign; the older meanings are shown for comparison and labelled as a
modern transfer. We found no Hellenistic or Arabic text in these sources that
reads the Moon's monthly passage through houses counted from the Sun sign,
and the records say so rather than inventing one.

The three highlighted periods' names (Reflection and preparation, Renewal
and expression, Resources and consolidation) are modern wording, ours, and
each member can change them.
