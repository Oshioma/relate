# Research batch 13: four more Hindu festivals on their real dates

Oshi asked (16 November 2026) for the festivals that the old lunar-day lines
mentioned but that had no date of their own to be added as dated events.

| Festival | Month and lunar day (amānta) | Checked at | Published date | Ours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phulera Dooj | Phalguna Śukla Dvitīyā | sunrise | 19 Feb 2026 | 19 Feb 2026 |
| Hala Shashthi (Lalhi Chhath) | Shravana Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī (Bhadrapada in the north) | sunrise | 2 Sep 2026 | 2 Sep 2026 |
| Vamana Jayanti (Vamana Dvadashi) | Bhadrapada Śukla Dvādaśī | midday | 23 Sep 2026 | 23 Sep 2026 |
| Kartika Purnima (Dev Deepawali) | Kartika Pūrṇimā | sunrise | 24 Nov 2026 (and 15 Nov 2024, Wikipedia) | 24 Nov 2026, 15 Nov 2024 |

Dating works as in batch 12 (`hinduFestivalDatesFor`, `HINDU_FESTIVAL_TIMES`).

## Sources and cautions
- Kartika Purnima: Wikipedia.
- The other three: Drik Panchang festival pages. Those pages don't load from
  our tools, so the dates were taken from search listings of them and from news
  sites citing them; the records say so. Before publishing, a reviewer may want
  to open each link and confirm the date.
- **Chandra Shashti** (named beside Balarama on the old tithi line) is not
  added: no source we could reach confirms its day. Hala Shashthi is the
  festival kept on that day, and its record notes that some traditions keep
  Balarama's birthday on Shravana Pūrṇimā instead.
