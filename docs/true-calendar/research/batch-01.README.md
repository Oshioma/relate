# The True Calendar — research batch 01

**43 research records:** 30 Hawaiian lunar nights, 5 selected Māori lunar-night examples, 8 dated Akan Akwasidae observances.

## Usage
- `research_batch_01.json` is an import candidate; `research_batch_01.csv` is a review-friendly export.
- All entries are `research_draft`, not published calendar recommendations.
- `ordinal` is a traditional night position, **not** a direct mapping to a Gregorian date.
- Hawaiian details summarize Kapiʻolani Community College's source compilation, which records disagreements among earlier writers.
- Māori examples are drawn from Te Papa's composite account; iwi variations must be preserved. The numbered Māori examples are approximate reference positions, not authoritative for every iwi.
- Akan Akwasidae dates are from a community 2026 schedule and should be checked with relevant community authorities.
- `modern_application` is editorial suggestion, **not** a historical claim or tested outcome.
- Do not derive Gregorian daily advice from these records until the relevant calendar mapping and location-specific rules have been verified.
