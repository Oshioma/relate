# Research: when did Iḍā and Piṅgalā begin crossing in pictures?

Status: **OPEN / earliest depiction not verified**. This is a visual-provenance investigation, not a claim that the earliest known illustration has been found.

## Established distinction
- **Textual precedent:** The medieval *Gorakṣaśataka* names Iḍā, Piṅgalā and Suṣumṇā, locates them left/right/centre and routes awakened Kuṇḍalī upward through Suṣumṇā (already in the timeline; PR #599).
- **Not established by that passage:** the modern alternating, repeated crossings at each chakra, a caduceus genealogy, or a DNA analogy. These need independently dated illustrations or explicit historical descriptions.

## Visual candidates and verification
1. **Wellcome Collection yogin painting, photographed at SOAS**. Commons full painting: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yogin_in_meditation_chakras_kundalini_snake.jpg ; detail: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kundalini_serpent_in_yogin%27s_belly.jpg . Described as a coiled Kuṇḍalinī serpent ascending the central channel with Iḍā and Piṅgalā **on either side**. This is *not* proof of the two lateral channels crossing each other. Original manuscript date, collection number and catalogue attribution need checking with Wellcome. Aggregators give contradictory broad ranges (1000–1500 vs 1840–1940); **do not adopt either as an object date**.
2. **Nāth Charit folio 2, attributed to Bulaki, 1823 (Samvat 1880), Mehrangarh Museum Trust**. Secondary image listing: https://jenikirbyhistory.getarchive.net/topics/subtle%2Bbody/kundalini . Obtain museum catalogue/folio image and verify whether it actually depicts crossing lateral channels, rather than merely chakras.
3. **Ajit Mookerjee, *Kundalini: The Arousal of the Inner Energy***. Reproduces historical artwork and describes opposite spirals. Treat his modern explanatory prose separately from the provenance and dates of each reproduced historical image: https://maypoleofwisdom.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/ajit-mookerjee-kundalini-the-arousal-of-the-inner-energy.pdf .
4. **Modern explicit crossing diagram**: Swami Satyananda Saraswati, *Prana and Pranayama* (available online): https://kaivalyam.org/assets/images/yoga-books-pdf/prana.pdf . It explicitly depicts and describes the channels alternating/crossing at chakras. Evidence for the *modern diagram*, not for its antiquity.

## Editorial rules
- Never call the Wellcome serpent **two intertwining Iḍā/Piṅgalā serpents**.
- Do not label the caduceus as derived from Indian nāḍīs without historical transmission evidence.
- Use image status **unverified** with caption beginning `UNVERIFIED —` if identification, date, or visual match is not confirmed.
- Avoid reusing the same full painting and detail as independent evidence.
- No earliest-date claim until an identified, dated primary image with **visibly alternating crossings** is inspected.
